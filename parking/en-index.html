How does booking work?

Booking is designed to be quick, without any needless steps.

Pick the date and time of your arrival at the car park and of your return. You see the price including the shuttle right away.

Contact details, licence plate and flight number. If you park on company account, enter the company ID – we fill in the billing details.

If you like, add covered VIP parking, car pick-up at the airport, a car check or cleaning.

The confirmation comes by email and text message, and we remind you the day before arrival. You pay only on site – by card or in cash.

Car park location
