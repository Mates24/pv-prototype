Where are you coming from?

The car park is in an easy-to-reach part of Bratislava, close to the D1 motorway.

D1

From the D1 motorway

Follow the D1 towards the city and take the Zlaté Piesky exit. From there you reach Stará Vajnorská 39 quickly and easily.

BA

From Bratislava city centre

Via Vajnorská or the nearby main roads towards Zlaté Piesky. Also easy to reach from the north and east of the city.

P

When you arrive

Park in the car park, pay on site by card or in cash, and our shuttle takes you to the airport. You keep your keys.

After you land

Call us once you have your luggage. We pick you up in front of the terminal and drive you back to your car.
